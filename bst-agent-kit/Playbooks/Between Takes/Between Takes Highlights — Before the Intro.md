## Shared highlights standard

<!-- Notion synced block (source: Between Takes Highlights — Before the Intro, https://app.notion.com/p/4166d55232fa81f3a416eedc447d0b6e#d25272ba8c2349cd856f08b92828ebf1) -->

**Episode order: HIGHLIGHTS → INTRO → CONTENT → OUTRO.** Place a short, truthful highlights hook before the approved Version 04 intro. Preserve the main conversation; copy excerpts into the hook.

- Choose compelling statements, surprises and authentic questions that make viewers want to learn more. Preserve meaning, context and complete words.
- Use motivated J cuts and L cuts with synchronized reaction coverage. Never overlap competing spoken lines or cut a word to force a picture change.
- Use fixed close, medium and wide crops at meaningful dialogue beats. Go tighter for emphasis or emotion; open wider for questions and shared reactions. Avoid slow animated pushes as the default.
- Use the Epidemic Sound plugin to choose music that fits the specific moment. Keep speech clear and preserve the approved natural dialogue treatment.
- **Hard picture cut from highlights into the intro is the default.** A dissolve was tried and rejected in the HP review. Shape music endings separately; keep the branded intro attack clear and avoid competing beds, clicks or chopped words.
- Keep the established intro-to-content music fade and gentle dialogue entry. Measure the red-dot intro cut from the intro's own start, after the highlights.
- Use teal **00 HIGHLIGHTS**, blue **01 INTRO**, orange **02 CONTENT**, green **03 OUTRO**, and purple graphics. Match clip labels and actual section ranges.
- Save a separate native revision in standard Premiere Pro, verify the opening with picture and sound, and present it for review. Preserve originals and prior revisions. An active export hold stays in force.

## 1. Gather the episode inputs

Read the latest direct feedback and current episode notes. Identify the latest saved native edit, synchronized multicam sources, approved dialogue mix, Version 04 brand assets, sequence frame rate and any review/export hold. Confirm the running app is standard Premiere Pro, never Beta.

Use the actual episode audio and picture to judge excerpts; transcripts help locate candidates but do not establish exact word endings, synchronization or subjective sound quality. If sources are unavailable, record the missing material and prepare only the supported selection notes. If the music plugin needs login, let the user complete authentication; continue independent editorial preparation.

## 2. Build curiosity without misrepresenting the conversation

Review the episode for a strong idea, surprising detail, emotional stake or question. Select excerpts that form one coherent promise for the viewer. Leave a meaningful question open rather than giving away the whole payoff. End on an authentic question when it serves the material.

Keep the hook as short as the ideas and natural delivery allow. HP's roughly 21-second assembly is an example, not a compulsory runtime or number of excerpts. Avoid disconnected sound bites, manufactured claims, misleading rearrangements and out-of-context reactions. Do not invent dialogue or remove qualifiers to make a claim stronger.

Record source time ranges, speaker, intended hook and why each excerpt belongs. Copy the excerpts; retain their original appearances in the main conversation.

## 3. Finish speech before trimming picture

Listen around the source handles and each new join. Preserve consonants, word tails, breaths and meaningful reaction space. Do not cut immediately at an estimated transcript endpoint. HP's “The paperless office is nonexistent” needed extra tail so the full word could finish.

Use very short audio ramps or matching room tone where needed to prevent clicks, without fading away a syllable. Keep conversational pauses intentional.

- **J cut:** start the incoming speaker's audio before cutting to that speaker's picture.
- **L cut:** let the outgoing line continue over an appropriate synchronized reaction or wider view.
- Start with subtle offsets and adjust by listening and watching. HP's six-frame prelaps at 24 fps are examples, not timing to copy blindly across episodes.
- Preserve lip-sync whenever a speaking mouth is visible. A reaction must come from the actual relevant moment or clearly supported coverage, not imply a response to something else.

## 4. Change perspective with fixed crops

Cut between stable close, medium and wide framings at idea changes, emphasis, questions or reactions. Hold each crop long enough to feel deliberate. Do not introduce continuous slow scale animation unless specifically requested.

Tighter crops should emphasize the statement or emotion. Wider coverage should give questions and shared reactions room. Use a real synchronized wide camera when it provides a meaningful perspective change. Check headroom, eyes, hands, microphones, look room, sharpness and frame edges throughout each crop.

HP v18 tightened three close views after feedback. Its nested-source scale values are project-specific; do not treat them as universal percentages. Judge the actual 1920 × 1080 picture and source resolution.

## 5. Select and mix music through Epidemic Sound

Search the installed Epidemic Sound plugin using the scene's actual mood, pace and emotional intention. Prefer an instrumental arrangement with space for dialogue when appropriate. Audition in context and keep its pulse and build supportive, without masking speech. If access or a required download is unavailable, report that dependency; do not invent a selected or licensed track.

Save the selected track and generated editing assets in the episode's protected Supporting Assets, with track title, artist and source recorded. HP used “Desert Dew” by Alec Slayne for its thoughtful technology/learning hook; it is not the required track for other episodes.

Keep the approved natural dialogue processing. Music selection does not authorize replacing the mix or adding aggressive denoise/compression. Check music and dialogue routing, stereo mapping and combined peaks.

## 6. Enter the intro, then the content

Finish the hook's last word and meaningful pause, then make a clean hard picture cut into Version 04. Keep the intro's own original music attack synchronized. Tailor the teaser bed's ending so the handoff is intentional; do not automatically carry a second music bed across the brand intro.

After the intro, retain the established red-dot picture cut, gradual intro-music fade beneath the episode and gentle dialogue entry. The HP reference is 00:00:02:22 at 24 fps, measured **from the intro's local start**, not the start of a sequence containing highlights. At other frame rates, use the same visual boundary and verify on a valid frame.

Keep the host/guest introduction and lower thirds in the main content's established title window. Adding a teaser shifts the full episode, titles, markers and outro together; it must not alter their internal synchronization.

## 7. Organize, check and hand off

Use the compact color-coded section scheme above. Keep underlying camera edits editable and retain necessary audio/music tracks. Use actual section boundaries rather than copying HP timestamps.

- [ ] The first material is the highlights hook, followed by intro, content and complete outro.
- [ ] Each excerpt has a clear purpose, creates interest and preserves the speaker's meaning.
- [ ] Every word is complete, including tails at joins; pauses feel natural.
- [ ] J/L edits are motivated, lip-sync holds and reaction coverage is faithful.
- [ ] Crop changes are static and purposeful, with clean framing and usable image quality.
- [ ] Music fits the moment, remains beneath speech and hands off cleanly to the branded intro.
- [ ] The hard intro cut, local red-dot boundary and intro-to-content audio fades are correct.
- [ ] The main episode, title window, source sync and ending are preserved.
- [ ] Native opening playback was checked with picture and sound; record who checked it and any checks still pending. Data, meters and screenshots alone do not establish a listening pass.
- [ ] Save the exact revision and cue the opening for review. Record feedback and approval separately from preparation.
- [ ] Export only when authorized and no active hold remains. Then run the existing actual-MP4 audio, picture, integrity and delivery checks on that exact file.

## 2. Follow the episode order

### Episode structure

1. **Highlights:** open with a curiosity-building excerpt sequence using the Highlights playbook; keep complete words and finish on an intentional beat.
2. **Intro:** hard cut into Version 04 with its original sound. End its picture at the approved local red-dot boundary, then fade its music beneath the podcast audio. Check for cropping or black edges.
3. **Content:** retain the main conversation. Copy hook excerpts rather than removing their original appearances. Preserve approved teaser material and music.
4. **Lower thirds:** during the established early wide shot in the main content where the guests and host are clearly identifiable.
5. **Outro:** after the complete end of the conversation, play the full outro animation and sound. No extra black or cut-off animation.

## 3. Join the intro, conversation and outro

### Intro and outro

- Unlink the intro video and audio in Premiere, keeping sync. Intro music and dialogue on separate tracks.
- Fade the intro music out gradually beneath the opening dialogue, and gently fade the podcast audio in as well. Treat the picture cut and the two audio fades separately. Keep the complete first word audible; avoid an abrupt music stop, a sudden voice entrance, or a dip into silence. An overlap of about **0.5–0.75 s** is a starting point, adjusted by listening.
- At the end, keep the complete final thought, sign-off, and meaningful reaction. Trim trailing silence and production chatter. Never cut the final word or rush into the outro.
- Place the outro at a natural closing beat. Adjust music entry, level, and any short fade or overlap by ear. Don't force an overlap that masks the final words.
- Listen through both joins with picture. Use short audio fades or room tone to prevent clicks and sudden background changes.

### Repeatable red-dot intro style

Established from Jackson's HP review on 2026-11-12. Repeat this picture-and-sound transition on future episodes using the same Version 04 asset.

1. Keep the intro's original animation and sound synchronized up to the picture cut.
2. End the intro picture at the red-dot boundary and cut directly to the opening podcast shot. The final HP instruction is **00:00:02:22 at 24 fps**: 70 frames from the intro’s own start, approximately 2.917 seconds. With highlights before the intro, add the highlights duration to find the global sequence cut. This supersedes the earlier 00:00:02:17 request.
3. Start the opening podcast picture and complete retained dialogue together at that boundary. Keep the conversation, camera cuts, titles and outro synchronized when moving the opening. Do not remove words to fit the intro.
4. Keep intro music and dialogue on separate tracks. Carry the music tail across the picture cut, lowering it smoothly beneath speech and fading it to silence. Gently fade the podcast audio in without hiding the opening syllable.
5. Listen from before the cut through the first complete sentence, with picture. Adjust the two fades together until the transition feels continuous and the voice stays natural.
6. Save a separate native revision and review it in standard Premiere Pro. Do not use Premiere Pro Beta for this workflow.

**Frame-rate handling:** the 24 fps timecode is the HP reference, not a frame number to copy into every frame rate. For other sequence rates or a revised animation, match the same visual red-dot boundary, round to a valid frame, and verify with picture. Preserve the episode's own frame rate.

**HP v09 reference settings — awaiting transition review:** music fades gradually from 1.75 s to 3.5 s, leaving approximately 0.583 s under the podcast after the 2:22 cut. Dialogue fades in over 0.25 s (6 frames at 24 fps). These are implementation starting points, not a requirement to reuse identical curves or durations regardless of the material. The requested style is established; the latest HP fade treatment has not yet received final listening approval.

**Export hold:** Jackson explicitly said “Dont export until I review.” Keep HP exports on hold until he reviews and authorizes the next step. Documenting the style does not lift that hold. On any episode, respect an active review/export hold.

This newer intro-ending instruction replaces the earlier default to play the full intro picture. The full-outro requirement remains unchanged.
